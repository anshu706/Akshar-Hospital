In early to moderate arthritis, biological therapies like PRP and hyaluronic acid injections can significantly reduce pain and delay surgery by several years. Be Prepared

Preparing for Your Visit

Everything you need to know to make your consultation or admission as smooth as possible.

🏥

For OPD Consultation

  • Arrive 10–15 minutes before your appointment
  • Bring all previous X-rays, MRI, CT, or blood reports
  • Carry a list of current medications
  • Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing
  • Bring your insurance / TPA card if applicable
  • Note down your symptoms and duration before the visit
  • A companion is welcome, especially for elderly patients
🩺

For Planned Surgery / Admission

  • Attend the pre-operative consultation as scheduled
  • Complete all pre-op blood tests and clearances
  • Fast for 6–8 hours before surgery (specific instructions from your surgeon)
  • Remove nail polish and avoid heavy jewellery
  • Arrange for a responsible adult to accompany you
  • Pack minimal personal items (toiletries, phone charger, comfortable clothes)
  • Bring insurance pre-authorisation documents
✨

For Dermatology / Cosmetic Procedures

  • Arrive with a clean, make-up-free face
  • Avoid sun exposure for 2 weeks before laser treatments
  • Discontinue Vitamin A / Retinol 2 weeks before procedures
  • Mention all current skincare products and medications
  • Inform of any known allergies or past reactions
  • Drink plenty of water the day before any procedure
