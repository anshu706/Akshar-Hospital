🚗 Getting Here

🚗By Car: Located on SG Highway service road, Bodakdev. Free parking for 100+ vehicles inside the campus.
🚌By Bus: AMTS Route 151 / BRTS – alight at SG Highway–Bodakdev junction. 5 min walk.
🛺By Auto/Cab: Tell the driver "Akshar Hospital, Bodakdev, near SG Highway." App cabs (Ola/Uber) drop at the main gate.
✈️From Airport: ~18 km via SG Highway, approximately 25–35 min by cab.
🚉From Station: ~14 km from Ahmedabad Railway Station, approximately 20–30 min by cab.
