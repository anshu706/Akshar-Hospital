Our Infrastructure

World-Class Facilities at Akshar Hospital

Designed for safety, comfort, and clinical excellence — every space at Akshar Hospital serves a purpose.

🏥 OT 💓 ICU 🚨 Emergency 🏃 Physio 🔬 Diagnostics 🛏️ Rooms 🌿 Waiting 🚑 Ambulance
🏥

Operation Theatre (OT)

Three fully equipped modular OTs with positive-pressure laminar airflow HEPA filtration, 4K HD arthroscopy towers, C-arm fluoroscopy, and advanced anaesthesia workstations. Designed to NABH and ISO 9001 standards.

  • 3 Independent modular OTs with dedicated scrub areas
  • Laminar-flow positive pressure ventilation (ISO 5 air class)
  • HD Arthroscopy tower (4K resolution, Stryker)
  • Navigation system for joint replacement
  • Integrated surgical lighting (shadow-free LED)
  • On-call anaesthesiology team 24/7
💓

ICU / High Dependency Unit (HDU)

8-bed ICU and 4-bed HDU with 24/7 dedicated intensivist cover, advanced multi-parameter monitors, mechanical ventilators, and a patient-family communication protocol to keep loved ones informed at all times.

  • 8 ICU beds + 4 HDU beds
  • 24/7 intensivist and trained ICU nurse coverage
  • Advanced haemodynamic & respiratory monitoring
  • Mechanical ventilators and BiPAP/CPAP
  • Central nursing station with real-time vitals display
  • Patient call system and relative communication protocol
🚨

Emergency & Trauma Ward

A dedicated round-the-clock emergency orthopaedic service with trauma resuscitation bays, ATLS-trained surgeons, and priority imaging. Walk-in and ambulance patients receive immediate triage and treatment.

  • 24/7 on-call orthopaedic resident & attending
  • 2 trauma resuscitation bays
  • ATLS (Advanced Trauma Life Support) trained team
  • Immediate X-ray and digital imaging
  • Minor OT for emergency wound care & casting
  • Direct escalation pathway to ICU and main OT
🏃

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centre

A comprehensive 1,500 sq ft rehabilitation centre with specialised post-surgical recovery programmes, staffed by BPT/MPT qualified physiotherapists. Targeted for joint replacement, spine, and sports injury recovery.

  • Underwater treadmill (aquatherapy)
  • TENS, IFT & EMS units
  • Therapeutic ultrasound
  • Wax bath & hot pack therapy
  • Gait training aids & parallel bars
  • Customised sports rehab protocols
  • Home physiotherapy programme guidance
🔬

Diagnostics & Imaging

On-site digital radiography for same-day bone and joint imaging, along with on-call radiology reporting. Seamless tie-ups for MRI, CT, and PET scans with same-day result availability for urgent cases.

  • Digital X-ray (DR) — same-day reporting
  • Portable bedside ultrasound
  • Dermatoscopy & trichoscopy unit
  • On-site pathology lab (blood & urine)
  • MRI / CT (tie-up centre nearby, same-day)
  • Bone densitometry (DEXA) referral available
🛏️

Inpatient Rooms

A choice of private, semi-private, and general ward accommodation — all designed for clinical safety and patient comfort, with 24-hour nursing care and attendant facilities.

Special Rooms

Private, AC, attached bath, TV, attendant sofa, fridge, nursing call button.

Semi-Special

2-bed AC room, shared bath, TV, nursing call button.

General Ward

6-bed ward, ceiling fans, locker, clean shared bathrooms.

All Rooms

24/7 nursing call system, clean linen, meals & dietary guidance.

🌿

Waiting Area & Reception

Our airy, plant-filled reception area is designed to calm anxiety and make your visit as comfortable as possible. Free Wi-Fi, dedicated seating, and a patient-assistance desk ensure you always feel supported.

  • Spacious, naturally lit waiting area
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi & phone charging points
  • Purified drinking water dispensers
  • Dedicated patient-assistance & billing desk
  • Wheelchair access & lifts to all floors
  • Kids corner with books & toys
🚑

Ambulance Service

ALS-equipped ambulances staffed with trained paramedics, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — for emergency response and inter-hospital patient transfers.

  • Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances
  • Cardiac monitor, defibrillator, portable ventilator
  • Trained paramedic crew on every trip
  • GPS-tracked fleet for real-time monitoring
  • Dedicated ambulance entrance & bay
