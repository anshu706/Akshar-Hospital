About Our Ortho Centre

Restoring Movement, Rebuilding Lives

Our Orthopedic Centre of Excellence is led by fellowship-trained surgeons offering a complete spectrum of care — from conservative management of sports injuries to complex revision joint replacement surgeries. We use navigation-assisted technology and HD arthroscopy to achieve superior outcomes with less pain and faster recovery.

Whether you suffer from a sports injury, chronic joint pain, a fracture, or a spinal condition, our team is equipped to diagnose accurately and treat effectively.

When to Consider Joint Replacement?

✓ Severe joint pain
✓ Difficulty walking
✓ Failed conservative treatment
✓ Advanced arthritis
✓ Bone-on-bone grinding
✓ Joint stiffness

Book Consultation →

Warning Signs — When to See a Spine Specialist

⚠️ Neck / back pain > 6 weeks
⚠️ Pain radiating to arms/legs
⚠️ Numbness or tingling
⚠️ Muscle weakness
⚠️ Bladder/bowel changes
⚠️ Difficulty walking

Joint Preservation & Biologics

For patients with early to moderate arthritis, we offer advanced biological therapies to preserve the natural joint, reduce pain, and delay or avoid replacement surgery.

  • Ozone Therapy for Joints
