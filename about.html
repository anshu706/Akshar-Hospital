Our Story & Mission

Founded in 2009 with a singular purpose — to bring world-class orthopaedic and dermatology care closer to the people of Ahmedabad.

Our Beginning

A Hospital Built on Trust

Akshar Hospital was founded in 2009 by Dr. Rajesh Patel and a small team of specialist doctors who believed that every person in Ahmedabad deserved access to the same quality of orthopaedic and skin care available in the country's premier metro cities — without the associated barriers of cost, waiting times, or intimidating hospital environments.

Starting with a 20-bed facility in Bodakdev, we have grown into a 60-bed multi-speciality hospital equipped with state-of-the-art operating theatres, a dedicated ICU, an advanced physiotherapy centre, and a full-service dermatology clinic. Through it all, our core values — compassion, transparency, and clinical excellence — have remained unchanged.

Today, having served over 50,000 patients and performed more than 12,000 procedures, Akshar Hospital stands as one of Ahmedabad's most trusted names in speciality healthcare.
